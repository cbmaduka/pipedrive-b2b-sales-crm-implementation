# Business Requirements

## Business Challenge

Nexora needed a repeatable sales process capable of supporting multiple B2B opportunities without relying heavily on manual tracking.

Without a structured CRM process, opportunities could remain unattended, follow-up activities could be missed, and management would have limited visibility into pipeline performance.

## Requirements

The solution needed to:

- Centralize B2B opportunities.
- Establish clearly defined pipeline stages.
- Capture qualification information.
- Track decision makers and decision timelines.
- Record expected deal values and closing dates.
- Create clear next activities.
- Automate repetitive sales follow-up.
- Monitor deal progression.
- Identify pipeline health.
- Provide sales-performance reporting.
- Track sales targets.
- Maintain standardized service offerings.

## Success Criteria

The implementation would be considered successful when a salesperson could:

1. Add an opportunity.
2. Qualify the opportunity.
3. Move it through a standardized sales pipeline.
4. Receive appropriate follow-up activities.
5. Monitor upcoming sales work.
6. Review pipeline and performance reports.
7. Compare results against defined targets.
