# Workflow Automations

Six sales workflow automations were implemented.

## 1. New Opportunity Qualification

Creates an initial qualification activity when a new opportunity enters the sales process.

## 2. Qualified Lead → Discovery

Creates a discovery activity after an opportunity reaches the Qualified Lead stage.

## 3. Product Demo Follow-Up

Creates follow-up activity associated with the product demonstration stage.

## 4. Proposal Sent Follow-Up

Ensures proposals do not remain unattended by creating a follow-up activity.

## 5. Negotiation → Follow-Up Call

Creates an appropriate follow-up during negotiation to maintain momentum.

## 6. Contract Review → Final Action

Supports the final pre-sale stage by creating an action associated with contract review.

## Automation Strategy

The automations were intentionally distributed across the sales lifecycle rather than concentrated at a single stage.

This creates a systematic sequence of next actions as opportunities progress toward closure.

## Business Value

The automation layer helps:

- Reduce forgotten follow-ups.
- Standardize sales execution.
- Improve accountability.
- Maintain opportunity momentum.
- Reduce repetitive administrative work.
