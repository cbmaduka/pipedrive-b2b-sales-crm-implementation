# Pipeline and Deal Management

## Nexora B2B Sales Pipeline

The following stages were configured:

1. New Opportunity
2. Qualified Lead
3. Discovery Call
4. Product Demo
5. Proposal Sent
6. Negotiation
7. Contract Review
8. Won

## Pipeline Logic

### New Opportunity
Initial sales opportunity requiring qualification.

### Qualified Lead
The opportunity has sufficient potential to continue through the sales process.

### Discovery Call
Nexora explores the customer's requirements, pain points and stakeholders.

### Product Demo
The proposed solution is demonstrated or presented.

### Proposal Sent
Commercial documentation has been provided to the prospect.

### Negotiation
Commercial terms and requirements are being discussed.

### Contract Review
The opportunity has reached final contractual consideration.

### Won
The sales opportunity has successfully closed.

## Deal Information

Deal records incorporate information such as:

- Deal title
- Organization
- Contact person
- Deal owner
- Deal value
- Expected close date
- Business need
- Product interest
- Decision maker
- Decision timeline
- Competitor information

This provides sales users with relevant context throughout the opportunity lifecycle.
