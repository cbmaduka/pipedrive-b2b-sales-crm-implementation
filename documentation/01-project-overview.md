# Project Overview

## Project
Nexora B2B Sales CRM Implementation

## Platform
Pipedrive CRM

## Business Scenario

Nexora is a fictional B2B technology and automation company providing workflow automation, enterprise integration, HR automation, analytics, and operational automation solutions.

The company requires a structured sales-management system capable of supporting opportunities from initial qualification through contract review and closure.

## Project Objective

The objective was to configure Pipedrive as a centralized B2B sales environment that supports:

- Opportunity management
- Sales qualification
- Pipeline progression
- Activity management
- Automated follow-up
- Sales reporting
- Revenue goal tracking
- Product management

## Implementation Scope

The project covers:

1. Sales pipeline design
2. Deal configuration
3. Qualification data
4. Sales activities
5. Workflow automation
6. Reporting and analytics
7. Dashboard configuration
8. Revenue goals
9. Product catalogue management

## Outcome

The resulting Pipedrive environment provides a structured system for managing Nexora's pre-sales lifecycle while improving visibility, accountability, and consistency across the sales process.
