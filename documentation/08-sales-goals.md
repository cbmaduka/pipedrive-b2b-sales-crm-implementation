# Sales Goals

A quarterly sales revenue target was configured for the Nexora B2B Sales Pipeline.

## Goal

**Nexora Q4 Sales Revenue Target**

The goal tracks deals won during Q4 2026.

## Purpose

Goal tracking allows sales management to compare actual performance against defined revenue objectives.

## Business Value

This introduces measurable performance management into the CRM and connects day-to-day opportunity management with broader sales objectives.

> Note: The portfolio environment reflects the account's configured regional currency settings for goal reporting.
