# Activity Management

Activities were incorporated into the Nexora sales process to ensure each opportunity has a defined next action.

## Activity Types

The implementation uses activities such as:

- Calls
- Meetings
- Tasks
- Follow-ups

## Examples

Activities can support:

- Initial qualification
- Discovery sessions
- Product demonstrations
- Proposal follow-up
- Negotiation follow-up
- Contract-stage actions

## Business Value

Structured activity management helps prevent opportunities from remaining unattended and provides sales users with a clear view of upcoming work.
