# Reporting and Dashboard

## Nexora Sales Performance Dashboard

A dedicated dashboard was configured to provide management-level visibility into the sales pipeline.

## Reports

### Deal Value by Stage

Provides visibility into opportunities across stages and expected closing periods.

### Nexora Deal Progress by Stage

Shows how deals are progressing through the sales lifecycle.

### Pipeline Health

Provides a stage-level view of pipeline movement and conversion.

### Deals Status by Salesperson

Provides a visual breakdown of sales ownership/status information.

### Activities Status

Shows the distribution and status of sales activities.

### Deals Won Revenue

Provides visibility into revenue generated from successfully closed opportunities.

## Visualization

Multiple visualization formats were used, including:

- Bar charts
- Stacked columns
- Pipeline visualization
- Pie charts
- KPI values

## Business Value

The reporting layer allows users to move beyond individual deal management and evaluate the overall health and performance of the sales operation.
